# Wed Shot Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-20_

Wed Shot is a Chrome extension for capturing screenshots of web pages.

**Wed Shot does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal or user data.**

- All processing happens locally in your browser. Screenshots stay in the extension page's memory until you save them to a file you choose, or close the tab.
- Wed Shot makes no network requests of its own and contains no analytics, tracking or remote code.
- Your viewport size and zoom preferences are saved only in your browser's local storage on your device.
- The `debugger`, `tabs`, `declarativeNetRequest` and host permissions are used solely to load the page you choose inside the capture workspace and to take the screenshot. They are not used to read, record or send your browsing activity.

If you have questions, please contact the developer via the support link on the Chrome Web Store listing.

# Wed Shot 隱私權政策

**Wed Shot 不收集、儲存、傳送、販售或分享任何個人或使用者資料。**

- 所有處理都在你的瀏覽器本機完成。截圖只存在擴充功能頁面的記憶體中,直到你存成你選擇的檔案或關閉分頁。
- Wed Shot 本身不發出任何網路請求,不含分析、追蹤或遠端程式碼。
- 視窗尺寸與縮放偏好只存在你裝置上瀏覽器的本機儲存空間。
- `debugger`、`tabs`、`declarativeNetRequest` 與網站存取權限僅用於在工作頁載入你選擇的網頁並擷取畫面,不會用來讀取、記錄或傳送你的瀏覽活動。
